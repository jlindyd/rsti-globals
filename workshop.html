By the end of the day, your team will:

  2. 02

    Integrate Lean and Robustness thinking

  3. 03

    Apply Energy Flow and Physics of Failure

  4. 04

    Front-load quality and reliability

  5. 05

    Improve manufacturing stability

  6. 06

    Enhance supply chain resilience

  7. 07

    Apply Predictive Health Management

  8. 08

    Energy Flow & Physics of Failure

    Deliverable: an Energy Flow Diagram.

  4. Session 4

    Frontloading Robust Product Development

    Deliverable: a Robust Design Opportunity Matrix.

  5. Session 5

    Predictive Health Management

  6. Session 6

    RVF in Manufacturing & Supply Chains

    Stability on the line and resilience across the network.

  7. Session 7

    Building a RVF Enterprise

    Making the framework the organization's default way of working.

  8. Session 8

    Reflection & Action Planning

    The day closes with your team's own 90-day RVF roadmap.
