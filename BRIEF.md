# RSTI Globals — Leading with Robustness™

Derived from the user-supplied site outline ("RSTI Globals / Leading with
Robustness™ — Website Outline") and the existing draft `RSTI-H_1.HTM`. The user
supplied full content, structure, and brand colours directly rather than
answering the Step 0 interview questions, then dismissed a follow-up
clarifying-questions prompt — read as: proceed and make the craft decisions.
This brief is **derived from that outline and the draft, with grammar,
signature move, typography and motion authored under implied creative
delegation.**

## 1. Vibe

Executive, engineering-serious, editorial. Not a consumer launch. References
implied by the source material: a Wiley engineering textbook, an executive
workshop deck, a scorecard — the register of a research-backed method being
taught to leadership, not a product being sold to consumers.

## 2. The scroll journey (Home)

In the user's own section order:
Hero (thesis) → Featured programs (masterclass, book) → Mission (four
principles) → Stats (real programme figures) → The RVF Framework (the flow
diagram) → Executive Learning Pathway (five levels) → Industries → Insights
preview → The Book → Collaborate (close).

## 3. Energy curve

Calm and declarative at the open, builds steadily through the framework
(the one moment of real visual intensity), then settles back into a quiet,
confident close. Never loud — the audience is engineering leadership, and a
loud page would undercut the credibility the content is trading on.

## 4. Feeling curve and peak

```
1  Resolve       the thesis stated plainly on a dark title page, gold tagline, no media
2  Curiosity     two real, concrete offers named (masterclass, book)
3  Conviction    the four-principle philosophy stated on a navy band
4  Confidence    real programme numbers (5 / 12 / 5 / 4) counting up
5  Awe           [PEAK] the RVF flow draws itself, node by node, under the reader's scroll
6  Clarity       the five-level pathway laid out as a structured climb
7  Recognition   the reader's own industry named in the list
8  Momentum      the insight series, a living publishing cadence
9  Trust         the Wiley book, the credentialed foundation under everything above
10 Resolve       a quiet colophon close: one line, one address, no shouting
```

**The peak** (act 5, Framework): the sentence a visitor would say to a friend —
*"the reliability framework actually drew itself in front of me, one stage at a
time, until the whole flow connected end to end."* It gets the largest scroll
span on the page, a quiet act (stats settling) in front of it, and the gold
accent reserved for its line work.

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where the framework draws itself
under your scroll instead of just being illustrated."

## 5. Signature move

The RVF flow diagram (Customer Needs → RVF → Lean Product Development → Energy
Flow Optimization → Robust Design → Built-in Quality → Lifecycle Reliability →
Supply Chain Resilience → Operational Excellence → Predictive Health
Management → Continuous Learning → Next Generation Product, from the Workshop
Agenda's "Robust Value Flow Framework") is drawn as a single SVG spine whose
`stroke-dashoffset` is driven directly off the pinned act's `--sc-p`, with each
of the twelve node labels arriving in its own progress window as the line
reaches it. Bespoke inline JS reads `--sc-p` off the act element every frame;
the engine is untouched. Under reduced motion the spine renders fully drawn and
every label is visible at rest, so the content survives, only the draw-on
motion is dropped.

## 6. Aesthetic range

Editorial (paper, folios, measure, restraint) — the source content is a
method/manifesto with real figures, meant to read as something the visitor
*read* rather than *watched*. Chosen over premium-minimal because the content
is dense and structured, not a single emotional arc.

## 7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes

Distinct chapters, hard-cut. The content outline is already a sequence of
discrete arguments (mission, stats, framework, pathway, industries, insights,
book) with no shared physical space to fly through, so chaptered editorial
(hard per-chapter grounds, no continuous drift) is the structural fit, not
worldflight.

## 8. Assets on hand

Brand colours and tagline only: navy `#0f1f38`, blue `#1c4c8c`, gold `#c8963e`.
Tagline "Create Value. Preserve Value. Sustain Value." No photography, no
footage, no logo file, no `KIE_AI_API_KEY` configured. This is a first-class,
zero-generation build: the page is carried by typography, real content, real
verified numbers (5 RVF stages, 12 insights/year, 5 learning levels, 4
quarterly webinars — all from the user's own figures), hairline structure, and
one bespoke SVG diagram built from markup, not imagery.

## Grammar: chaptered editorial

Chosen over the other seven:
- **Filmic one-shot** needs a full-bleed scrub hero; there is no footage.
- **Live surface** fits a running product demo; RSTI sells a framework and
  education, not software.
- **Continuous world** needs real geography to fly through; nothing here is a
  physical place.
- **Typographic poster** bans photographic ground and cards outright and
  wants type to carry the whole page; this content is structured and
  tabular (levels, agenda, scorecard) and needs real layout, not pure scale.
- **Gallery/catalog** fits equal objects with museum labels; RSTI's five
  learning levels are a sequence/argument, not a walkable collection of
  equivalent items.
- **Split stage** needs one binary comparison as the whole spine; nothing
  here is a two-sided argument.
- **Rhythmic cutlist** is an energy-brand grammar; wrong register for an
  engineering-executive audience that wants to feel it read something
  substantial.

Chaptered editorial fits: long-form substance, a founder-authored method, real
figures, hard per-chapter grounds instead of a continuous world, no fixed nav
bar (a folio marks the chapter instead), and a colophon-style close instead of
a shouting CTA band.

## Silence, authored

The stats chapter (4) is deliberately quiet — plain numbers, no media, short
stagger — so the framework diagram immediately after it has something to be a
change from.

## Scope note

This brief and build cover the **Home page** as the scroll-driven flagship.
The remaining eight pages in the user's site map (Framework, Programs,
Executive Workshop, Roadmap & Scorecard, Insights, Book, About/Partnership,
Collaborate) carry the same design tokens, folio nav and hard-cut chapter
grounds, but are built as reference/administrative content — per feel.md §5,
"compress the administrative parts... flow sections at short stagger, not
pinned acts with dwell" — since their job is to hold dense tabular content
(the full workshop agenda, the scorecard) rather than carry an emotional arc.
