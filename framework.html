03 — Six Domains

One system, six domains.

  1. 01

    Customer Value

    The need the whole flow exists to serve, named plainly before any design work begins.

  2. 02

    Engineering

    Robust design and energy-flow thinking, built in before the first prototype, not tested in after.

  3. 03

    Manufacturing

    Stable, standardized processes that hold up under real variation on the line.

  4. 04

    Reliability

    Lifecycle performance engineered in, tracked across the full product life, not just at launch.

  5. 05

    Supply Chain

    Resilience against disruption, designed into the network rather than reacted to after it.

  6. 06

    Learning

    Field experience and predictive health data feeding directly into the next generation of design.

04 — Lean vs. Robustness

Two disciplines, one framework.

Lean Thinking

  • Process flow
  • Waste reduction
  • Standard work

Robustness Thinking

  • Energy flow
  • Variation reduction
  • Built-in quality

RVF integrates both: Lean Thinking improves process flow, Robustness Thinking improves energy flow.
