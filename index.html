03 — Principles

Variation is inevitable. Robustness is engineered.

Lean removes waste. Robustness strengthens performance.

Lean improves flow by removing waste from a process. Robustness Thinking goes further: it examines how sensitive that process is to variation—in people, materials, equipment, demand, and environment—so the improvement still holds once conditions change.

“Lean removes the fat. Robustness builds the muscle.”

For example: removing an inspection step saves time—a Lean win. But unless the process is also made less sensitive to supplier variation, quality can slip once that inspection is gone. Robustness Thinking is what lets the saving stick.

  1. 01

    Build Robustness Early

    Desensitize products and processes to unavoidable variation during development.

  2. 02

    Standardize What Works

    Standardize what repeatedly delivers safety, quality, and performance.

  3. 03

    Continuously Improve

    Use Lean, Kaizen, and lifecycle feedback to strengthen performance.

  4. 04

    Learn for What's Next

    Turn field experience and predictive health insights into better designs.
