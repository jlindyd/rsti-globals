Experience You Can Count On

Our distinct approach integrates quality and business management systems to help improve your bottom line cost savings and business growth. 03 — Robustness

Robustness is a feature in many systems, natural and artificial alike. We investigate robustness from a perspectives of bus system and its application in modern environments. A focus investigates the relevance and importance of robustness in a discipline where many techniques are inspired by problem-solving strategies found in nature—artificial intelligence. The challenging field of artificial intelligence provides an opportunity to engage in a wider discussion about robustness.

The importance of robustness as a ‘principle’ cannot be underestimated. For instance, reliability, availability, safety, and security are important requirements in many systems.
